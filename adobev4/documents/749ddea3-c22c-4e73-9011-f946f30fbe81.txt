 
2.Digital Signature  
A digital signature ensures the authenticity and integrity of a message or document 
in digital communication.  
How It Works : 
○ The sender encrypts the message using their private key to generate a 
unique signature.  
○ The receiver uses the sender's public key to decrypt the signature and 
verify its authenticity.  
○ This process ensures the message is sent by the claimed sender and 
has n ot been tampered with.  
Applications : 
○ Used for secure transactions.  
○ Proves identity in online communications.  
Additional Concept : 
○ Double Encryption : 
■ Combines encryption and signing for enhanced security.  
■ Example: Encrypt with a public key, sign with a priva te key.
